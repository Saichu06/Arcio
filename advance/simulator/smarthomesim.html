    How To Run This Mission

    Manual

    A quick walkthrough — from picking a mission to deploying the finished smart home. Everything below maps directly onto the panels of this page.

    01

    Pick a mission

    Choose one of the 8 mission chips in Mission Control. Each targets a different engineering challenge — night automation, cooling, storm protection, gas & fire response, security, energy saving, and leak protection.

    02

    Wire the hardware

    In the Arduino Control Center, flip switches to wire the Lights, AC/Fan, Curtain, Lock, Pump and Siren relays. Required devices are flagged — leaving one unwired blocks that automation.

    03

    Set the automation rules

    Tune the light threshold and AC trigger sliders, and leave rain-safe closing and emergency auto-response switched on unless you're deliberately testing a failure.

    04

    Press Run Simulation

    The mission clock starts, live weather drives the house, and the Arduino begins making its own decisions each tick based on sensor readings.

    05

    Watch the digital twin

    Windows glow, curtains move, the garage and camera react, and colored particles flow from sensors → Arduino → devices. Click any sensor dot to inspect its live reading.

    06

    Inspect the hardware

    Hover the house, garage, solar roof, garden, tank or camera for live engineering data. Double-click the Arduino board to open the fullscreen GPIO visualizer.

    07

    Stress-test with events

    Use the event buttons — Night, Heatwave, Storm, Gas Leak, Smoke, Intruder, Water Leakage, Power Failure — to throw curveballs and see if your wiring holds.

    08

    Review & deploy

    When the mission ends, a summary shows your automation, security, comfort and AI scores. Click Explain for reasoning, then Deploy Smart Home to keep it running live.

    Good To Know
    Live TelemetrySix sparklines track power, indoor temp, humidity, battery, solar output and water usage tick-by-tick.
    IoT Cloud Status widgetThe floating card bottom-right shows Arduino connectivity, packet count and latency — tap its header to collapse it.
    Ambient soundThe speaker icon in the top bar turns on wind, rain, relay clicks and the siren — off by default.
    Progress gaugeThe ring next to the title tracks overall mission completion — same number as the bar in Mission Control.
    Data-flow particlesBlue is routine telemetry, orange is a command from the Arduino, red means an emergency is active.
    Reset anytimeUse Reset in the console to rewire from scratch without reloading the page.
